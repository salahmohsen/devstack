# System label taxonomy (Career Inbox)

## job_notifier

**Intent:** bulk or curated open-role promotion → **job harvest workflow**.

Examples (positive):

- LinkedIn “Your job alerts” with multiple View job cards
- Jack and Jill “4 new senior frontend roles (Paires, …)” with job links
- Indeed / Google job alert digests

Examples (negative):

- “Application was sent to MixRank”
- Recruiter: “Would you chat next week about a Staff role at …”

## application_status

**Intent:** update on an application already in flight → **CRM stage/outcome path**.

## application

Receipts and portal notices that should surface under Applications without being a job digest.

## recruiter / interview / offer

Human process stages for the seeker’s candidacy.

## noise

Should not clutter Needs action / career cards.
